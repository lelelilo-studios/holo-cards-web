# Lelelilo Holo Cards

**Holo Series 01 · Stitched in Light**: twelve lelelilo pillows, painted, stitched into holographic trading cards and
sealed in a booster pack. Tear it open, then tilt every card until the foil catches the light.

**Open: https://lelelilo.com/holo-cards/** (also at https://lelelilo-studios.github.io/holo-cards-web/)

Each card is a small 3D model with its own painted layers, foil pattern and back. Drag to tilt it, **F** flips it over,
**R** straightens it. Lumina's card is lenticular: tilt her sideways. Works in any browser with WebGL, on phones too.

## How they were made
- Each lelelilo was painted by Codex's image tool from its own 3D model on the lelelilo.com island, so fabrics, ears
  and accessories match.
- Every card went through [holo-card-studio](https://github.com/EverettFish/holo-card-studio): four painted layers
  (character, background, line art, text), one Blender scene, one little 3D model for the browser.
- The page is a sheet from the lelelilo.com sketchbook. Poke Lumina five times.
